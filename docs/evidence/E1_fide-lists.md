# E1 — FIDE's monthly lists, February 2015 to October 2026

Status: REVIEW — evidence for the proposal (§1, §2) and annex T8 (session ELO-3, Phase 4.1). Generated by `analysis/e1_fide_lists_report.py` from `analysis/aggregates/E1_standard.json`, `E1_rapid.json` and `E1_blitz.json`, which `analysis/e1_fide_lists_extract.py` computes from FIDE's lists; the lists are analysed and never committed or redistributed [V 3]. Do not edit by hand. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (`docs/LICENSE-docs.md`).

## Source and definitions

- FIDE's monthly standard, rapid and blitz lists, 2015-02 to 2026-10: 141 lists each, with 597,369, 491,918 and 326,775 distinct FIDE IDs rated at least once [V 3].
- Anchor cohort (PROVISIONAL), for a 12-month window from list s to list s + 12: aged 25 to 45 in the calendar year of s, rated and not flagged inactive on both lists, with at least 10 rated games in the window and at least 10 in the 12 lists up to s. Drift is the median of R(s + 12) − R(s), also split at a rating of 2000 on list s.
- Windows that contain the March 2024 list are left out: on that list FIDE raised every rating below 2000 once, by 0.40 × (2000 − R), and moved the floor from 1000 to 1400 [R §2].
- Newcomer: an ID on a list and on no earlier list since February 2015, counted from February 2016. Exit: an ID on one list and absent from the next.

One list stands out: 18,130 IDs left the standard list in April 2026 (the twelve lists before it lost 534 to 1,942 each). 15,712 of them had first been listed in March 2026, when the list gained 20,922 newcomers. The regulations remove a rating when it falls below 1400 (§7.2.1 [V 1]), but 14,134 of these players were rated 1450 or more on the March list, so a fall below the floor in one month explains few of them. The lists do not say why; this is recorded as a FIDE-side event for the architect.

On the published lists there is no excess of players at the floor: on the October 2026 standard list 230 players are rated exactly 1400 against 545 to 760 at each of the next nine points, and the number per band rises from 1400 upwards in all three lists. The research report describes "an artificial pile-up at 1400" after the reform [R 3] [R 4]; a list cannot show players below the floor, who leave it (the exits above), so the two observations can both hold.

## Limits

- The lists carry no games: drift here is the movement of published ratings for a cohort whose strength is assumed stable (adults aged 25 to 45), not a measurement of strength. The anchor of annex T4.5 needs Layer 1.
- The cohort excludes players flagged inactive and requires ten games in each of two 12-month windows; 2020 and 2021 have small cohorts.
- Newcomers are IDs new to the lists since February 2015, with a one-year burn-in: a returning player first rated before 2015 and absent since counts as new.
