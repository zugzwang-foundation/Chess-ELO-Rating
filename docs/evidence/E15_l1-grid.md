## 1 What R41 asks

Layer 1's two hyperparameters, c_θ (the scale of the monthly random walk of a player's strength) and ω (the innovation SD of the time-control offsets), both SPEC-L1 §3.7, were chosen on held-out games (§4.6): first c_θ at a fixed ω, then ω at the chosen c_θ. Both choices sat at an end of their grids (`analysis/OUTPUT_L1_history.md`). R41 (`docs/decisions/D-0011_rulings-and-freeze-3.md`, reading 15) widens each grid by two of its own steps beyond that end and searches again in the same way; if the optimum moves inside the wider grid, E6's rung 5 test is rerun with it and rung 5's history figures restated, and if it stays at an edge, the documents say so. The championship's frozen rung-5 inputs stay as frozen either way.

Protocol: fit 2023-01 to 2024-09, held-out score 2024-10 to 2024-12, three-outcome log-loss; the widened search takes c_θ in {2.0, 4.0, 8.0} at ω = 4.0, then ω in {1.0, 2.0, 4.0} at the chosen c_θ.

## 3 What R41 then requires

The optimum stays at (2.0, 4.0), now inside both widened grids, so Layer 1's history fit (`analysis/OUTPUT_L1_history.md`), E6's rung 5 test and the championship's frozen rung-5 inputs (`params/rung5_us2026.json`), each of which reads its hyperparameters from `analysis/aggregates/L1_history.json`, use the values the widened search confirms: nothing is rerun, and rung 5's history figures stand as E6 reports them [E6].
