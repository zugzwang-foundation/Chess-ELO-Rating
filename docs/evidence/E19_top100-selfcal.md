**In brief.**

- **The self-calibrating system starts in 2025.** Its first table is 2025's (E18 §1: no earlier year has a window of games with FIDE IDs); before it the system uses FIDE's rules, so the two systems agree on every list to December 2024 and differ only through tournaments rated with the 2025, 2026 tables.

## 1 What is replayed

As E17 (`docs/specs/SPEC-TOP100_v1_0.md`): the 100 highest-rated active players of FIDE's October 2026 standard list (rank 100 at 2626), each from the January 2020 list, every standard game FIDE rated for them on the lists of February 2020 to October 2026 from FIDE's published per-player calculations, the 100 replayed together month by month, the opponents outside the 100 at their published ratings (the approximation R58 tests), and the difference the self-calibrating replay minus Layer 0's. With these changes (SPEC-SELFCAL §10):

- **the table**: each tournament uses the table of the year of the list in force at its start [E18 §2]: the 2025 table for lists of 2025, and so on; η(L) rounded to whole points in each band, the published entry to three decimals, the narrowed guard as frozen (applies: True), no 400-point rule; before 2025, table 8.1.2 as FIDE applies it;
- **K**: FIDE's K rules applied to each system's own rating (R61, SPEC-SELFCAL §9);
- **colours** as E17, from the broadcast archive; a game without a matched colour carries no colour term.

## 7 K (R61)

FIDE's K rules in the order SPEC-L0 R-22 settles, applied to FIDE's own published ratings (with each player's history from the February 2015 list, 30 games or more), reproduce FIDE's printed K on 8,200 of 8,200 player-lists; where they do not (0), the self-calibrating K is FIDE's. Applied to the self-calibrating ratings, the rules change no tournament's K: every player of the 100 crosses the same thresholds at the same lists under both systems.

## 8 Limits

- **Two years of tables.** The self-calibrating system has tables for 2025, 2026 only [E18 §1]; the difference accumulates over the 22 lists rated with them, not E17's 81.
- **Opponents outside the 100** keep their published ratings (R58's range in §3); a replay of the whole pool needs FIDE's tournament reports (proposal section 9, stage 2).
- **The table's own error** at the top is bounded, not removed (§3, R59).
- **Two scales.** A flatter table spreads ratings further apart at the top (annex T3.5), so counts above 2700 and 2800 compare two scales as well as two rules.
- **Colours** come from broadcasts; a game without a matched colour carries no colour term [E17 §8].
- **The rules** of 1 March 2024 to 30 September 2025 for the 400-point rule are read as the plain cap, NOT VERIFIED against the handbook; FIDE's calculations agree in every tournament that can tell [E17 §2] (SPEC-L0 §11).
- **Not anyone's true strength.** The figures show what the rule does to ratings with the same results.
