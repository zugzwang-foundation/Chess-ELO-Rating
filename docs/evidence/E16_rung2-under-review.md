## 6 R24's rule (i) and the guard's reach

Rule (i), read strictly (D-0011, reading 4), keeps no guard only if the 95 % interval of the favourite's residual in the farming region lies inside ±0.01. The interval's width depends on the games, not on the residual: with the region's per-game spread s (its cluster-robust standard error times √n), the interval fits only from n = (1.96 s / (0.01 − |r|))² games, r the residual:

| time control | games in the region | cluster-robust SE | s | interval width now | games needed, r = 0 | at the observed r | with the guard: players' interval, inside ±0.01? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| standard | 827 | 0.00705 | 0.203 | 0.028 | 1,579 | 4,512 (r = +0.0041) | -0.0071 (-0.0210 to +0.0056): not significantly outside |
| rapid | 1,010 | 0.01171 | 0.372 | 0.046 | 5,319 | 52,588 (r = +0.0068) | -0.0414 (-0.0651 to -0.0195): significantly below |
| blitz | 1,020 | 0.00779 | 0.249 | 0.031 | 2,380 | never (|r| ≥ 0.01) | -0.0016 (-0.0167 to +0.0133): not significantly outside |

- **Reach.** The region needs a level of 2300 or more and a gap of 400 or more, so a favourite rated 2500 or more; full weight needs 2575 (a gap of 450 at a level of 2350). In standard 193 of the 827 region games carry full weight, and the guard binds in 806 [E12].

## 8 Title thresholds under K × m

A title needs a rating achieved at some time, which may be obtained in the middle of a rating period or of a tournament (Title Regulations §1.5.3 a) [VT 1]). Simulated: a player whose strength equals the rating plays 9 games a month for 3 months against opponents of the same rating and strength, colours alternating; results drawn from the v2 table (the truth), the expectation read from the published v2 table, the interim rating the published rating plus the period's game terms so far, the period's change rounded once (§8.3.4 [V 1]); the share of 40,000 runs in which the interim or the published rating reaches the threshold, with K and with K × m (the same results for both):

| starting rating (= strength) | K | threshold | rung 2 with K | rung 2 with K × m | m of the band |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2470 | 10 | 2500 | 0.054 | 0.171 | 1.49 |
| 2480 | 10 | 2500 | 0.203 | 0.400 | 1.49 |
| 2370 | 20 | 2400 | 0.340 | 0.468 | 1.44 |

Each share has a standard error of at most 0.0025. Under rung 2 at today's K the interim rating moves by K × m a game, so thresholds are touched more often without playing better.
