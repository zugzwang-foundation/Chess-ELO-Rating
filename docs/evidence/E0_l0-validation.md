## Method

The Layer-0 engine (`src/layer0`, SPEC-L0 v1.0, ratified in D-0006) rates the event with `next_list`, the October 2025 list serving as both the list in force and the previous list. Each game's Delta R is compared with the change FIDE prints for that game, each player's sum, K and K x sum with FIDE's calculation for the event, and the new rating with the November 2025 list. Two players also played the 2025 Grand Chess Tour Finals in the same period; for them FIDE's figures for that event are added before the period's change is rounded once (R-25), and the alternative of rounding each event's change is shown beside it.
