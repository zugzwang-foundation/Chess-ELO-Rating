## Fixed by the pairings and the October ratings (R55)

*fixed by the pairings and the October ratings — the same whatever the results.*

Per player, (b′) − (a) = K × Σ(E_a − E_b′) and (b0) − (a) likewise: the score cancels, so each is set by the pairings, the October 2026 ratings and K, all known before round 1 (review finding ELO7-STAT-1). So is the PILOT's difference, K × Σ(E_b − E_p) over a compensated junior's games against opponents who are not eligible: on (b′) at today's K (R56), and on (b) with K × m (withdrawn, reported here once, superseded). Column (b) is not set by the pairings: m multiplies the score, so (b) − (a) = C + K × Σ (m − 1) × score over the player's games, and the pairings fix only C, the value with no point scored, and C + W, the value with every game won (W = K × Σ (m − 1)); where the result falls between them waits for the results. The figures assume every game is played; a game marked not played (D-0013) removes its term, the only way results can move them. They are computed by Freeze 3a's own code on two copies of each event, every game given a placeholder result (all draws; all wins for White), which must agree exactly; no result is read. The rounded changes, the players whose rounded change differs, (b)'s blanks and the forecast test depend on the results and are not computed.

## The forecast test (R54), pre-registered in D-0014

For every counted game of both events (D-0013): the three-outcome log-loss, −ln of the probability the forecast gave the result (nats), and the expected-score Brier score, (S − E)², of column (a)'s expectation (table 8.1.2 as FIDE applies it, Layer 0, on the October 2026 ratings) and of column (b′)'s (Freeze 3's v2 table with its narrowed guard and its colour term). Table 8.1.2 has no draw model, so (a)'s three outcomes split its expected score with the draw rate of the game's 100-point level band in the v2 table's training months, standard broadcast games of 2023-10 to 2026-09 (`params/draw_rate_2026-10b.json`, E2's construction); (b′)'s split its expectation with the v2 model's own draw probability at the game. Each game is scored from White's side. Reported: the two means and their difference (b′) − (a), a negative difference favouring (b′), with percentile bootstrap 95 % intervals (2,000 resamples, seed 20261009) over games and over players, each resampled within its event, per event and pooled. The sample is small (at most 66 games an event, 132 in all): the difference may favour either column, or neither, and it is reported whatever it shows.

The draw rates behind (a)'s three outcomes at the level bands of the two fields' games: 2200–2299: 0.318; 2300–2399: 0.370; 2400–2499: 0.440; 2600–2699: 0.575; 2700–2799: 0.596.
