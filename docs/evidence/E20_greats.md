## 6 (iv) The cross-era head-to-head: not attempted

No legitimately usable game data link the eras (DATA-PROBE §5; SPEC-GREATS §7): TWIC, ChessBase, Chessmetrics and the other game collections need permission; OlimpBase's Olympiad games credit ChessBase's database and TWIC's editor among their sources; the Lichess broadcast archive starts in 2020; FIDE's calculations give each player's own games from 2008 only. A comparison of one era's greats with another's would need every game between them and the players linking them, rated on one scale; the data that would allow it are not ours to use without permission. With permission from TWIC, ChessBase or Chessmetrics the full study becomes possible.

## 7 Limits

- The lists before 2001 are OlimpBase's transcription of FIDE's lists (checked against FIDE's own files of 2001 only); the ranks before July 2000 are computed by SPEC-GREATS §2's rule, not printed by FIDE.
- The replay covers the lists of April 2008 to December 2019 only, the window in which FIDE's calculations give every game; most of the greats' careers lie outside it, and the greats of the 1970s and 1980s are not replayed.
- Opponents outside the set keep FIDE's ratings (R58's approximation); ±10 points around them is shown in §5.
- The colour term uses FIDE's colour marks, unchecked before 2020.
- The 2025 table is applied backwards to an era it was not fitted on: the replay shows the rule's effect on the same results, not a measure of anyone's strength, and not a comparison across eras.
- The rounding before July 2014 is read from FIDE's pages (R74), a reading changed after partial pages were seen; the rounding of July 2014 to December 2019 keeps a reading FIDE's pages contradict for negative half points, and those periods are thin (§4.2).
