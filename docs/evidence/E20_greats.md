## 4 (iii) The replay, April 2008 to December 2019: validation first

**What is replayed** (SPEC-GREATS §6). ## 6 (iv) The cross-era head-to-head: not attempted

No legitimately usable game data link the eras (DATA-PROBE §5; SPEC-GREATS §7): TWIC, ChessBase, Chessmetrics and the other game collections need permission; OlimpBase's Olympiad games credit ChessBase's database and TWIC's editor among their sources; the Lichess broadcast archive starts in 2020; FIDE's calculations give each player's own games from 2008 only. With permission from TWIC, ChessBase or Chessmetrics the full study becomes possible: the replay could extend to the games those collections hold. FIDE's own calculations, against which a replay is checked, exist only from 2008; those collections do not record which games FIDE rated; and any comparison across eras would rest on a model's assumptions, not on the ratings themselves.

## 7 Limits

- The lists before 2001 are OlimpBase's transcription of FIDE's lists, NOT VERIFIED before 2001 (checked against FIDE's own files of 2001 only); the ranks before July 2000 are computed by SPEC-GREATS §2's rule, not printed by FIDE; before 1999 the ratings are multiples of five, so ties for 3rd bring in six players (§2), while from July 2000 FIDE's printed order breaks ties where a printed copy survives (R82), a shared place still counting on the 23 lists computed without one; FIDE's lists of 1994, as OlimpBase transcribes them, lack the player ranked 1st on the lists either side (§2).
- The replay covers the lists of April 2008 to December 2019 only, the window in which FIDE's calculations give every game; most of the greats' careers lie outside it, and the greats of the 1970s and 1980s are not replayed.
- Opponents outside the set keep FIDE's ratings (R58's approximation): the mean difference moves by 0.93 points per point of theirs (§5), and a replay of the whole pool would move them too.
- The colour term uses FIDE's colour marks, NOT VERIFIED before 2020 (E17 checked them against the broadcast archive from 2020 only).
- The 2025 table is applied backwards to an era it was not fitted on, and on these games, for the players together, the favourites' results sit nearer table 8.1.2, an order that depends on the mix of players chosen before and in the window and that a sample chosen for success could reverse (§5): the replay shows the rule's effect on the same results, not a measure of anyone's strength, and not a comparison across eras; its fit window holds few games at the top levels where the greats play.
- Layer 0's rounding follows FIDE's output, towards +∞ to the list of July 2018, which R80 extends to October 2018, and away from zero from November 2018 (R74 before July 2014, a reading changed after partial pages were seen; R80 from July 2014, set after the full replay had been read): the reading of the texts' Rn as the rated player's new rating and of the 2007 text's half point, the text in force in 2018 and the list on which FIDE's arithmetic changed that year are NOT VERIFIED; the list of January 2009, which rounded its negative half points away from zero, keeps those periods thin (§4.2, §4.3).
