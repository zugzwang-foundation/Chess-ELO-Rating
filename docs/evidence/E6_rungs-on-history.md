## 1 Protocol

- **Origins.** 21 test months, 2025-01 to 2026-09. At each month t, Layer 1 is fitted on the months before t (36 at most, starting from the archive's first month 2023-01), warm-started from the previous month's fit, with the hyperparameters chosen in the history fit (c_θ 2.0, ω 4.0; `analysis/OUTPUT_L1_history.md`). Every quantity used at t comes from that fit and from the list of month t; nothing from month t's games or later enters it.
- **Fits.** Games per fit 243,166 to 735,427, players 41,603 to 85,963, sweeps 26 to 65; the anchor's final shift at most 15.70 points.
- **Layer 0.** FIDE's expected score from table 8.1.2 with the 400-point rule and, in standard from October 2025, the 2650 exemption (`src/layer0/`), on the published ratings and K of the list in force; three-outcome scores split it with the draw rate of the game's level band in the 12 months before (annex T8.3, as E2).
- **Intervals.** Paired moving-block bootstrap by month, blocks of 3, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261009 (annex T8.5). "Not significantly outside ±b" means the 95 % interval overlaps [−b, +b]; across rating bands Holm–Bonferroni is applied to one-sided bootstrap p-values against the nearer bound (T8.2).
- **Do no harm** (every rung, T8.2): the upper end of the 95 % interval of the log-loss difference at most 0.002 nats a game, and the game-weighted mean absolute calibration residual over 50-point gap bins at most 0.005 worse than Layer 0's, measured on the games the rung changes (stricter than on all games, where unchanged games add zeros).

Meeting R15 on gaps that move 3 to 5 points a year needs a faster controller (a narrower deadband or a larger share of the excess) or a criterion on the settled gap: the PROVISIONAL constants are for the architect. The panel's d_t rests on Layer 1's estimates from broadcast games only, so these months test the controller's arithmetic, not its behaviour on FIDE's pool; rung 6 stays under TEST ON FIDE DATA (R22).

## 7 What the data cannot support, and the data that would

- **Juniors and newcomers below 2000.** In standard, 2,659 of 81,759 active rated juniors aged 18 or less have a usable Layer 1 estimate from broadcast games (OUTPUT_L1_history §5); rungs 3 and 5 are tested on the few strong juniors and newcomers who play broadcast events. The data that would settle them: FIDE's tournament reports (TRF, §9.1 of the rating regulations [V 1]), which hold every rated game.
- **K and the gates.** σ, K_i, the compensation gates and the seed gates are computed on broadcast games only, a fraction of each player's rated games: σ and K_i are biased upward and fewer juniors pass the gates than FIDE's record would allow.
- **Rung 6 under drift.** The test months fall in a period with almost no drift of the level; the controller's behaviour under sustained drift is measured in the simulator [E9] and needs FIDE's record to be settled.
- **Rung 7 and the farming region.** Federation offsets are not tested here (Phase 5 of the session tests only the direction of federation residuals); rung 7 and the formal farming-region test need FIDE's game archive (annex T8.1, R12).
