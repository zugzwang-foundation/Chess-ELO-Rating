# E8 — K from FIDE's activity record: rung 4 redesigned

Status: REVIEW — evidence for the proposal (§5, §9) and annex (T4.3, T8.1, T8.10), session ELO-5, Phase 1. **In brief.** Rung 4 does not pass with K built from FIDE's activity record. The certainty comes from the games field of every monthly list since February 2015, not from broadcast games, and K falls with the period's games (R16). On the months and rules E6 used, the forecasts of the players whose K changes most are worse by 0.00042 nats a game (interval -0.00125 to +0.00173), which does not meet the targeted rule, and the do-no-harm check is met (overall +0.00029 (-0.00021 to +0.00068)). E6's version, with σ from broadcast games, was worse by 0.00242 (interval +0.00066 to +0.00414) and failed both. Established players rated 2600 or more receive a median K of 13.3 in the test months (E6: 19.0; today 10); established club adults rated 1600–1999, who play a median 10 rated games a year, receive 30.3 for a one-game period and 23.7 for nine games (today 20). Rung 4 therefore joins TEST ON FIDE DATA (R22): it no longer harms forecasts, but it does not yet improve them measurably on broadcast games.

## 1 What changed from E6

- **Certainty from the lists.** σ_i is a Glicko-style variance in latent units built from FIDE's monthly lists alone: each period's rated games add q²·v_tc of information per game (v_tc the published table's score variance at equal ratings in the player's band), and every month adds c² = (c_K·p(age))², with p the age profile of SPEC-L1 §3.7, up to the newcomer's 250 (SPEC-K-ACTIVITY §3). Broadcast games never enter σ_i.
- **K falls with the period's games (R16).** K_i(n) = clip(q σ_i² / (κ_tc (1 + n q² σ_i² v_tc)), 10, 40), with n the period's rated games from the next list; in the test months n had a median of 8 (p10 2, p90 17).
- **Everything else is E6's.** The months, the population (the players E6 could give a Layer 1 estimate), the carry and forecast steps, the targeted group, the bootstrap and the decision rules are E6 §3's; the harness functions are imported unchanged from `analysis/e6_rungs_extract.py` (SPEC-K-ACTIVITY §6).

## 5 Reading

- **Why E6's rung 4 failed, and what the redesign fixes.** With σ from broadcast games most players looked uncertain and sat at K = 40; with σ from every rated game on FIDE's lists the K of established players falls to about today's level or a little above it (elite median 13.3 against E6's 19.0), monthly changes no longer double, and the forecasts are no worse than Layer 0's (do no harm met).
- **Why it does not pass.** The targeted rule asks for forecasts measurably better than today's for the players whose K changes most. The difference is within noise overall and in standard and rapid, and worse in blitz, where the chosen growth is largest. A K from activity alone cannot see whom a player met or how the results went: it rises for players who play rarely, such as most club adults, whatever their stability, and the lists cannot show whether that is warranted.
- **What would settle it.** FIDE's tournament reports (the TRF archive): opponents and results for every rated game, so that the certainty can count each game's information and be tested on the whole pool, juniors and club players included, rather than on the broadcast population (annex T8.6).

## 6 Limits

- The broadcast population is stronger and more international than the rated pool [E2]; the test covers it only.
- The lists count games, not opponents: each game is counted at equal strength, which overstates the information of games against much weaker or stronger opponents (SPEC-K-ACTIVITY §9).
- The recursion has no drift; juniors' improvement enters only through the larger growth of the young age bands.
- The games field counts games rated in a period, which can lag the games themselves.
- c_K was chosen on 2023–2024 broadcast months, which hold fewer games than 2025–2026 (the archive grows from 2024): standard 62,868, rapid 3,035, blitz 1,988 forecast games, so the rapid and blitz growth rests on little data, and blitz, whose chosen growth is the largest, is where the rung's forecasts are worse.
