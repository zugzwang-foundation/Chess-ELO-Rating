It reruns E9's scenarios and seeds (`docs/evidence/E9_simulator.md`) with the v2 table (E11) as the pool's true outcome model and as Layer 1's proxy's model; rung 2's ledgers read the published v2 table, with the narrowed guard (R24, E12) and K times the printed slope ratio m(L) (R32); rung 4's K is fixed per event (R25); accrual ends three months after the last rated game (R33). Parameters calibrated as in E9 and PROVISIONAL everywhere; section 9 lists what rests on assumptions.

Runs: 5 seeds for each of 5 scenarios, each ten simulated years after a three-year burn-in under Layer 0, with about 10,553 active rated players at adoption and 12,803 after ten years, about a twentieth of FIDE's 221,130 active rated standard players (`analysis/OUTPUT_L1_history.md`); every rung on the same simulated games as Layer 0 (pairings from Layer 0's list). Figures are means over the seeds, with their range in brackets. R1's threshold uses 20 further paired runs (section 8).

**In brief.**

- **The simulated Layer 0, on a pool whose true outcome model is the v2 table (flatter than table 8.1.2 near equal ratings in every level band, by R32's factor m, 1.31 to 1.82, most at the top), so that table 8.1.2's over-prediction of favourites is part of the simulated world:** the adults aged 25–45 whose true strength was 2400 or more at adoption lose 7.9 points a year on the list while their true strength changes by +0.1 (FIDE's lists since the 2024 reform: players rated 2200 or more lose 5 to 8 [E5]), and adults rated 2400 or more score 0.036 a game below today's expectation against juniors (broadcast games: 0.072 [E6]); today's newcomer rule publishes newcomers far above their true strength (section 2).

- **The slide at the top:** 7.9 points a year under Layer 0, 3.7 with rung 2 v2 (the narrowed guard, K × m), 3.5 without the guard, 2.4 with rungs 2 to 6 together (a negative slide is a rise).
- **The level:** from the first year to the tenth Layer 0's anchor cohort drifts -28 points against true strength in the baseline and -101 under a junior wave; rung 6 holds it to -2 and -58. Rung 3 alone moves it -69: today's newcomer rule injects points that offset the junior drain, and accurate seeds remove that offset, which is why rung 3 needs rung 6 beside it.

- **R1's review threshold:** the noise of the twelve-month mean of the spread ratio has an SD of 0.0053; the smallest threshold with at most 5 % false alarms from noise is 0.015 (0 of 20 runs; exact 95 % upper bound 14 %), which a ratchet at κ's annual cap trips after a median of 32 months; R20's PROVISIONAL 0.02 gives 0 % false alarms and trips on a ratchet after 38 months. Under R31 and R40 the review needs κ at its annual cap as well, which the simulated baseline never reaches; the ratchet then triggers at θ_R1 = 0.01 in 100 % of runs, after a median of 24 months (section 8).

## 1 What is simulated

- **Pool.** 20,000 players at the start in six federations (relative sizes 20 : 10 : 5 : 2 : 1 : 0.5), juniors 35 %, true strengths around 1450 (juniors) and 1750 (adults); entries 2.2 % of the active pool a month with E1's age shares, their true strengths around the 2023 newcomers' median first ratings moved to today's scale by the March 2024 compression, R + round(0.4 × (2000 − R)) [E1] [E5]; exits by age, activity and experience.
- **Strength.** A latent strength moving each month by Layer 1's fitted drift by age (`analysis/OUTPUT_L1_history.md`), multiplied under 25 by a personal factor of mean 0.45 (coefficient of variation 0.5), and a random walk of 12 latent points a month (15 after 45), E8's choice for standard; outcomes from the v2 table [E11] at the true strengths on the published scale, so White's edge, the slope and the draw rates by level are the v2 table's; Layer 1's proxy uses the same model.
- **Games.** Activity rising with strength (about 10 rated games a year for club adults, 50–60 at 2600 [E5] [E8]); 85 % of events domestic; Swiss events (9, 7 or 5 rounds) and round-robins of 10.
- **Ledgers.** Layer 0 exactly (an integer port of SPEC-L0, tested against `src/layer0/`), and each rung alone on the same games: rung 2 v2 with the narrowed guard and without it, both with K × m (R32); rung 3; rung 4 from the activity record (E8's design) and from Layer 1's certainty, each with K fixed per event (R25) and with the v2 table's κ(L) and v(L) in the player's band (annex T4.3); rung 5; rung 6 with accrual ending three months after the last rated game (R33); rung 7; and rungs 2 to 6 together, whose rung 4 sets K, so without m. Rung 1 is Layer 0 itself, so the RECOMMENDED NOW package is rung 2 v2 with the narrowed guard and K × m. Rungs 3 to 7 use a forward-filter proxy of Layer 1 that knows the pool's average dynamics (SPEC-SIM §5).
- **Scenarios.** Baseline; the baseline with twice the noise (Layer 1's history fit, c = 2.0); deflation with a junior wave; two isolated federations whose ratings start 100 too low and 60 too high; adversaries (section 7); and, for R1, a ratchet of κ at its annual cap.

- **Rung 6 holds the level in the baseline** (-2 points from the first year to the tenth against Layer 0's -28) and meets R30's rule there in 91 % of months. - **Rung 7 cannot reach isolated federations:** its offset is shrunk by n×/(n× + 2000), and a federation that plays 1 % of its games abroad has too few cross-border games for the shrinkage to let any adjustment through, so its offset decays at Layer 0's pace. That is the shrinkage working as designed: the evidence for an offset is the cross-border games themselves (annex T2.5).

## 11 Limits

- A synthetic pool about a twentieth of FIDE's active list, in standard only, five seeds a scenario: differences of a few points between rungs are within the seeds' range.
- Pairings follow Layer 0's list for every rung (the shadow-list design), so a rung's effect on who meets whom is not simulated.
- The ledger identity of annex T6 is not evaluated line by line; the points entering, leaving through the floor and posted by adjustments are reported in the aggregates.
- SPEC-SIM §11 lists the revisions the pilot runs forced and the two the v1.0 red team found (entrants' scale, chain-linking), each with its reason; the runs reported here follow all of them, and `src/simulator/v2.py` lists what this rerun replaces.
