## 1 The games

**2016 to 2019.** No free historical database could be used legitimately: The Week in Chess is "free for personal use only", with all rights reserved; PGN Mentor's files carry a copyright notice and no licence; KingBase states none (SPEC-SELFCAL §2, checked on 2026-10-11).
