## 5 Limits

- **Two tables, not seven.** The data allow tables for 2025, 2026 only (§1); the test of self-calibration rests on one year in which the year's table and the frozen first table differ.
- **Short windows.** The first table's window is ten months, cut at the March 2024 compression; the second's is 22. A window under 22 months makes no choice of form (SPEC-SELFCAL §5).
- **The caps** bind the second table to within annex T7.1's yearly changes of the first, whatever the data say; a parameter on a cap's bound is shown as such (§2), and the intervals of §4 treat it as fixed, so they understate that table's uncertainty.
- **Six selection months.** The rule of choice compares candidates on six months with blocks of three, so its bootstrap interval rests on few distinct resamples; on the confirmation months, not used to choose, the chosen form scores better than the table in force (§2).
- **The broadcast population.** Broadcast events are stronger and more international than the rated pool; the tables describe that population until FIDE's tournament reports test the method (annex T8.6).
- **Standard only.** Rapid and blitz are not fitted: their earlier rules are not transcribed.
