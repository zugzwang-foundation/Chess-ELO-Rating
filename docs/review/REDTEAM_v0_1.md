# Red-team review of proposal v0.1 against the ratified architecture (AR-1 to AR-6)

Status: REVIEW RECORD · Session ELO-2, Phase 1 · Date: 2026-10-09 · Reviewers: three independent subagents briefed as R-STAT (rating-system statistician), R-QC (Qualification Commission member and arbiter) and R-EXPLOIT (strong player seeking rating without playing better). Each read `docs/proposal/ELO-PROPOSAL_v0_1.md`, the architecture of D-0003 and the verification sheet `docs/research/VERIFICATION_2026-10-09.md` §1–2, and returned at most ten ranked findings. The full texts are summarised here; the consolidated status column says how v0.2 treats each finding: **fixed** (where, in the annex `docs/proposal/ELO-TECHNICAL-ANNEX_v0_2.md` or the proposal), **justified** (kept as the architecture specifies, with the reason), or **open** (carried to the close-out and to `docs/review/REDTEAM_v0_2.md`).

Totals: 30 findings; 5 blockers, 17 majors, 8 minors. Blockers and majors: 20 fixed, 2 justified, 0 open. Minors: 4 fixed, 4 justified.

Reviewers' PROVISIONAL assumptions differed from the values adopted in v0.2 (R-EXPLOIT assumed σ_new = 200, a_cap = 1; R-STAT proposed σ* ≈ 72); gains quoted below are the reviewers' own figures under their assumptions and are not re-computed here.
