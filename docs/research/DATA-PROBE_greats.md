### 2.5 The rating regulations in force

FIDE's handbook online holds these versions of the rating regulations:

- `B022024`: FIDE Rating Regulations effective from 1 March 2024
- `B022022`: FIDE Rating Regulations effective from 1 January 2022 till 29 February 2024
- `B022017`: FIDE Rating Regulations effective from 1 July 2017 till 31 December 2021 (with amendments effective from 1 February 2021)
- `B022014`: FIDE Rating Regulations effective from 1 July 2014 till 30 June 2017

Nothing earlier than 1 July 2014 is online at FIDE. The Internet Archive holds FIDE's earlier handbook: §8 of the regulations ("The working of the FIDE Rating System", http://www.fide.com/fide/handbook.html?id=73&view=article) in 55 answered copies from 2010-06-09 to 2025-02-19, and the handbook of the 2000s (http://www.fide.com/official/handbook.asp?level=B02..) in 22 answered pages from 2003-01-27 to 2007-10-14. Version 2014 (`B022014`) was fetched once at 2026-10-11T10:44:08Z for Phase 3; the older texts are to be transcribed from the archived copies, with their URLs and snapshot times, where the replay needs them.

## 5 What this allows, under the brief's rules (no analysis yet)

- **(i) FIDE's published series**: at every list date from July 1971 (OlimpBase's transcription of FIDE's lists to 2000, FIDE's own files from January 2001), aggregates only.
- **(ii) Dominance** (the gap to the list's #2 and #10): on every full list from July 1971; the supplementary lists of 1978–1980 are not full lists.
- **(iii) The replay under both systems**: only where FIDE's calculations give every game, from the list of April 2008; the tournament totals of July 2001 to January 2008 cannot carry a per-game table.
- **(iv) The cross-era head-to-head**: not possible. No legitimately usable game data link the eras: TWIC, ChessBase, Chessmetrics, PGN Mentor, KingBase, Chessgames.com, 365Chess and Chess-Results need permission; OlimpBase's Olympiad games credit ChessBase and TWIC's editor among their sources; the Lichess broadcast archive starts in 2020; FIDE's calculations give each player's own games from 2008 only, not a pool linking eras.
