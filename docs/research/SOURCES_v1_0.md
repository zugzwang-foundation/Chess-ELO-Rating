## 1 Why, and how the terms were read

**Why.** Our game evidence comes mostly from strong players: the broadcast games' typical rating is 2113, against 1734 for the rated pool [E2]. Yet Ghita reports that K = 40 players account for more than half of FIDE's game activity, 54.6 % (p. 73), and the game data behind his figures were shared with him privately by members of FIDE's Qualification Commission (pp. 62–64), so they are not ours to take. The session takes the cheap sources that bear on the four problems of the proposal (countries priced differently; White moves first; the top drains; fast improvers rated behind their strength) and pilots one larger source, FIDE's per-player game pages, so that the founder can decide from numbers whether a large fetch is worth it.

**How the terms were read.** Every site fetched in this session is FIDE's: ratings.fide.com, www.fide.com, fide.com and qc.fide.com. Each host's robots.txt was requested before any other page of it, on 14 October 2026 at 09:33:54Z to 09:33:58Z; each answered HTTP 404, publishing none, so no path is excluded (the answers are cached under `data/raw/sourcing/robots/`). Each site's published terms were then read from its own pages: the footer of ratings.fide.com (09:34:54Z) and of www.fide.com (09:34:59Z), the footer of qc.fide.com (09:35:03Z) and FIDE's privacy policy (https://www.fide.com/privacy, 09:35:17Z). Every request of the session is logged in `data/fetch_log/ELO-D1.tsv` (UTC time, host, path, status, size, SHA-256), and its counts and gaps are reported in [E21]. For sources not fetched in this session the terms are those read before, cited with their dates: DATA-PROBE §3 (`docs/research/DATA-PROBE_greats.md`, 11 October 2026) and the verification sweep of prior work [VP 8] [VP 9] [VP 10] (10 October 2026); they were not read again.

**FIDE's terms, as read on 14 October 2026.**

- ratings.fide.com and www.fide.com, footer: "No part of this site may be reproduced, stored in a retrieval system or transmitted in any way or by any means (including photocopying, recording or storing it in any medium by electronic means), without the written permission of FIDE International Chess Federation." (© 2026 on ratings.fide.com, © 2025 on www.fide.com; the same words.)
- qc.fide.com, footer: "Copyright © 2026 Qualification Commission. All rights reserved."
- FIDE's privacy policy, §11.1.1: any visitor of FIDE's website may see a rated player's name, year of birth, federation, gender, rating, title, inactivity flag and "history of games" (paraphrased; the quoted words are the policy's). §5.2 records that minors' consents are given by their legal tutors.

So FIDE publishes each player's game history to any visitor, and reserves storing it: as before (DATA-PROBE §2.6; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 6), FIDE's pages are analysed from a local cache that is never committed, and FIDE's written permission is to be requested. Many sampled players are minors: nothing that names or identifies a sampled player leaves `data/` (founder, 14 October; item 31).

**The data rule** (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 43). Where this project's material shows a named player's FIDE rating, it is FIDE's published rating, credited to FIDE; no FIDE list file is published. FIDE IDs are committed only in the test fixtures that quote FIDE's published calculations and the evidence report printed from them, for the players of the U.S. Championships, in their event files and the reports printed from them, and for the named players of today's top 100 and the greats; no sampled player's name or FIDE ID is committed.

## 5 Session ELO-D2: what was fetched, the deletion and the manifest

- **What was fetched.** The robots.txt of ratings.fide.com once more, on 14 October 2026 at 14:25:05Z (HTTP 404: no rules); then the club-level sample's per-player calculation pages of 2025 standard periods, by the plan committed before the first request (`docs/specs/SPEC-SOURCING_v1_1.md`), from one process with the pilot's tool, headers and User-Agent, every request logged in `data/fetch_log/ELO-D2.tsv`: 5,464 requests to ratings.fide.com from 14:25:05Z to 22:55:50Z, at least four seconds apart, no HTTP 429 or 5xx, one network error; the counts and gaps are [E21] §11.1's. The terms are those read on FIDE's sites in session ELO-D1 the same morning (§1), not read again. Nothing was requested of FIDE or of anyone else (item 34).
- **The deletion** (item 35). Once the session's analysis was verified (E21 regenerated from its aggregates, the tests and the automated check passing, the privacy scan clean), every cached calculation page of ELO-D1's pilot (2,000) and of ELO-D2's run (5,462) was deleted, on 15 October 2026 at 00:42:45Z, by `tools/data/fetch_sourcing.py` (mode delete-pages), after each page had been checked against the SHA-256 its log records (7,462 of 7,462). No per-game file had been written: the extractions read the pages and write totals only. E17's and E20's caches of calculation pages were not touched.
- **The manifest.** `data/raw/sourcing/MANIFEST` (local, never committed; mode manifest) lists each deleted page's link, period, session, fetch time, size and SHA-256, so that anyone can fetch it again and check it.
- **What remains in `data/raw/sourcing/`:** the robots.txt answers, ELO-D1's terms pages and Sonas's reports, the two samples' plans and queues (FIDE IDs and periods), the run logs and the manifest; the request logs stay in `data/fetch_log/`. All of it stays out of the repository.
- **Where `data/` lives.** The clone sits in a folder that the operator's machine keeps in iCloud Drive, with local copies evicted when space runs short; the cached pages were therefore held in iCloud Drive too, and a deleted file stays in its "Recently Deleted" folder for up to 30 days. Emptying that folder, and keeping `data/` out of iCloud Drive, are the operator's to decide (ELO-D2's close-out). Updated 15 October 2026: the operator has since moved the clone out of iCloud Drive.
- **The probe's tournament reports** (session ELO-15c, item 44). The probe's 20 tournament reports, which listed each event's players by name and FIDE ID and no game, were deleted on 15 October 2026 at 11:56:02Z by `tools/data/fetch_sourcing.py` (mode delete-reports), after each had been checked against the SHA-256 that ELO-D1's log records (20 of 20), read no further than that checksum; the manifest lists each with its link, fetch time, size and SHA-256.
