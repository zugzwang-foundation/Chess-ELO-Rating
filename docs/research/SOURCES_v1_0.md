## 1 Why, and how the terms were read

**Why.** Our game evidence comes mostly from strong players: the broadcast games' typical rating is 2113, against 1734 for the rated pool [E2]. Yet Ghita reports that K = 40 players account for more than half of FIDE's game activity, 54.6 % (p. 73), and the game data behind his figures were shared with him privately by members of FIDE's Qualification Commission (pp. 62–64), so they are not ours to take. The session takes the cheap sources that bear on the four problems of the proposal (countries priced differently; White moves first; the top drains; fast improvers rated behind their strength) and pilots one larger source, FIDE's per-player game pages, so that the founder can decide from numbers whether a large fetch is worth it.

**How the terms were read.** Every site fetched in this session is FIDE's: ratings.fide.com, www.fide.com, fide.com and qc.fide.com. Each host's robots.txt was requested before any other page of it, on 14 October 2026 at 09:33:54Z to 09:33:58Z; each answered HTTP 404, publishing none, so no path is excluded (the answers are cached under `data/raw/sourcing/robots/`). Each site's published terms were then read from its own pages: the footer of ratings.fide.com (09:34:54Z) and of www.fide.com (09:34:59Z), the footer of qc.fide.com (09:35:03Z) and FIDE's privacy policy (https://www.fide.com/privacy, 09:35:17Z). Every request of the session is logged in `data/fetch_log/ELO-D1.tsv` (UTC time, host, path, status, size, SHA-256), and its counts and gaps are reported in [E21]. For sources not fetched in this session the terms are those read before, cited with their dates: DATA-PROBE §3 (`docs/research/DATA-PROBE_greats.md`, 11 October 2026) and the verification sweep of prior work [VP 8] [VP 9] [VP 10] (10 October 2026); they were not read again.

**FIDE's terms, as read on 14 October 2026.**

- ratings.fide.com and www.fide.com, footer: "No part of this site may be reproduced, stored in a retrieval system or transmitted in any way or by any means (including photocopying, recording or storing it in any medium by electronic means), without the written permission of FIDE International Chess Federation." (© 2026 on ratings.fide.com, © 2025 on www.fide.com; the same words.)
- qc.fide.com, footer: "Copyright © 2026 Qualification Commission. All rights reserved."
- FIDE's privacy policy, §11.1.1: any visitor of FIDE's website may see a rated player's name, year of birth, federation, gender, rating, title, inactivity flag and "history of games" (paraphrased; the quoted words are the policy's). §5.2 records that minors' consents are given by their legal tutors.

So FIDE publishes each player's game history to any visitor, and reserves storing it: as before (DATA-PROBE §2.6; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 6), FIDE's pages are analysed from a local cache that is never committed, and FIDE's written permission is to be requested. Many sampled players are minors: nothing that names or identifies a sampled player leaves `data/` (founder, 14 October; item 31).
