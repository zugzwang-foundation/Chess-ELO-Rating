## Decisions

1. **The table's parameters are the broadcast fit.**
   - κ, η, α, β and γ of all three time controls are replaced by the maximum-likelihood fit on the last 36 months of the Lichess broadcast archive, 2023-10 to 2026-09 (standard: κ 1.2120, η 35.91, α 0.2860, β 0.4985, γ 0.2915).
   - They are labelled PROVISIONAL-FITTED, with the parameter file as provenance.
   - `analysis/v03_calculations.py` reads them from that file, so the documents' numbers follow the file.
   - Every other parameter stays PROVISIONAL: E1 and E2 do not estimate K bounds, compensation, the adjustment or Layer 1.
2. **η is rounded to whole points** (36, 37, 27). The published table has one row per whole-number gap (annex T3.4, unchanged).
3. **The fit evaluates the level at the midpoint of its 100-point band**, as the published table does (`docs/specs/SPEC-TABLE-FIT_v1_0.md`). Annex T3.3 now says so.
4. **Every worked example and derived figure is recomputed** with the fitted values, and the documents carry the new numbers:
   - the T3 properties, table excerpt and band steps;
   - colour, K steady states and junior–junior creation;
   - examples (i), (ii) and (iv) of T10 (example (iv) is the synthetic ledger month, which now closes at +1782);
   - the 8.1.1 comparison of T11.
   - The largest step between adjacent level bands rises to 0.012 in the published table (it was 0.009).
5. **D1's PROVISIONAL default γ = ½ is replaced by the fitted γ.** It is inside [0, ½] in every time control (0.29, 0.20, 0.31): the favourite's draws fade more slowly than its losses. The texts that said "as fast as" now say "more slowly than", with the numbers.
6. **The spread finding is stated through the local scale, not κ alone.**
   - The fit gives κ = 1.21 with a draw term that rises with level. The near-equal-strength scale 200(2 + ν_0)/κ is 481, 649 and 856 at levels 1700, 2300 and 2700, against logistic Elo's 400: a flatter table, as Sonas's "too spread out" predicts, flattening with level.
   - T3.5's sentence "the fit returns κ < 1" is replaced accordingly.
   - The illustration θ̃ − R = (1 − s)(m_t − R) keeps s = 5/6 as an illustrative spread factor.
7. **Evidence is cited as `[E n]`.** Check (c) verifies that `docs/evidence/E{n}_*.md` exists.
   - CLAUDE.md's hard rule 3 names `docs/research/` as the evidence base.
   - The evidence reports are generated by committed scripts from documented sources, and the brief orders them into the proposal.
   - Whether rule 3 should name `docs/evidence/` explicitly is left to the architect.
8. **Annex T8 gains T8.10.** It records what has been evaluated under D12:
   - rung 1 against FIDE [E0];
   - rung 2 passing on broadcast games in all three time controls, with the farming region inconclusive [E2];
   - rungs 3 to 7 not yet testable.
   - The rung rows of T8.1 carry the measured status.
9. **The proposal body stays within its cap** (4,463 words) **and the summary at 250 words.** The measured figures sit in §2's table rows, outside the strict count.

## Consequences

- REDTEAM_v0_3 and D-0005 are records and are not edited: their numbers describe v0.3 before the fit.
- Next time the table is refitted (yearly, T3.3), the same script and report regenerate the parameter file, and the documents' numbers follow from `analysis/v03_calculations.py`.
