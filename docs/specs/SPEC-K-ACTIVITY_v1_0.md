# SPEC-K-ACTIVITY — K from FIDE's complete activity record (rung 4 redesigned), v1.0

**Status: REVIEW — written before the code (ELO-5 brief, Phase 1).** Author: The Zugzwang Authors · Licence: CC BY 4.0 (`docs/LICENSE-docs.md`) · Date: 2026-10-10. Implements rung 4 of the adoption ladder (annex T4.3) with ruling R16 and the executor's readings 2 and 3 of `docs/decisions/D-0009_architect-rulings-elo-5.md`, taking the certainty σ_i from FIDE's monthly lists instead of from Layer 1's broadcast fit. Code: `src/layer2/kactivity.py`; tests: `tests/test_kactivity.py`; evidence: E8, generated by `analysis/e8_k_activity_extract.py` and `analysis/e8_k_activity_report.py`. Every parameter below is PROVISIONAL.

## 1 Purpose

E6 tested rung 4 with σ_i from Layer 1, fitted on broadcast games only. Those are a fraction of most players' rated games, so most players looked uncertain and sat at K_max = 40, and forecasts got worse (`docs/evidence/E6_rungs-on-history.md` §3). FIDE's monthly lists record, for every rated player, how many rated games each period held. This specification builds a certainty from that record alone: a Glicko-style variance that shrinks with each period's games and grows with each month that passes, from which K_i(n) follows by R16. It is then tested exactly as E6 tested rung 4. If it passes, it becomes rung 4; if not, rung 4 joins TEST ON FIDE DATA (R22).

## 2 Inputs

### 2.1 FIDE's monthly lists

The converted standard, rapid and blitz lists, `data/interim/fide/<tc>/YYYY-MM.tsv` (`tools/data/convert_fide_lists.py`), from February 2015, the first list archived [V 3], to the list in force for the period computed, and never later than October 2026. Per player and list: whether the player is rated, the published rating, the games field (rated games in the period ending at that list, §7.1.2 [V 1]) and the year of birth (the latest non-zero one on any list, as `analysis/fide_panel.py` reads it). The federation field is not read (decision D4). Results are not read: the recursion of §3 uses counts only.

### 2.2 The published table

κ_tc, α_tc and β_tc from `params/table_fit_2026-10.yaml` (read only; Freeze 1). The per-game score variance at equal ratings in level band L is v_tc(L) = 1/(2(2 + ν_0(L))), ν_0(L) = exp(α_tc + β_tc · (L_mid − 2000)/400), L_mid the midpoint of the 100-point band of the player's published rating on the list in force (R6; D-0008, reading 5).

### 2.3 Broadcast games

Layer 1's game records (`docs/specs/SPEC-L1_v1_0.md` §2.1, `src/layer1/data.py`), January 2023 to September 2026, for two purposes only: choosing the growth scale c_K on months before the test (§5) and the test itself (§6). The certainty σ_i never uses a broadcast game.

### 2.4 The data cutoff

No list later than 2026-10 is opened; no broadcast file later than 2026-09 is opened and every game dated after 2026-09-30 is dropped (`src/layer1/data.py`); the code raises an error if a later list is requested. No game of the 2026 U.S. Championships, which begin on 9 October 2026, can be read.

## 3 The certainty recursion

All variances are in latent units (annex T1), as R6 and R16 take σ_i. q = ln 10/400.

- **Information per game.** I = q² · v_tc(L), with L the band of the player's published rating on the list in force for the period. It is the Fisher information that one game at equal strength carries about the latent gap (annex T4.3). The lists do not record opponents, so every game is counted at equal strength.
- **Growth per month.** c = c_K,tc · p(a), with p(a) the profile of SPEC-L1 §3.7 by age band, (25, 25, 20, 14, 12, 15, 15) points a month for the bands under 12, 12–15, 16–19, 20–24, 25–45, 46–60 and over 60, the age being the list's calendar year minus the year of birth, and a player without a year of birth counted as 25–45 (annex T2.2). c_K,tc is chosen by §5.
- **First appearance.** At the first list on which the player is rated in tc, or the first list after one on which the player was not rated (a re-entry after the floor, §7.2.1 [V 1]), the variance after that list's games is P = 1/(1/s_0² + max(N_seed, G) · I), with s_0 = 250 (annex T2.2), N_seed = 5 (the minimum games for a first rating, §7.1.4 [V 1]) and G the list's games field. A player already rated on the first archived list, February 2015, starts instead from P = s_list² with s_list = 120 (SPEC-L1 §3.4), a rated player of unknown history.
- **Every later list t on which the player is rated.** P⁻(t) = min(P(t − 1) + c², s_0²), then P(t) = 1/(1/P⁻(t) + G(t) · I), with G(t) the games field of list t and I evaluated in the band of the rating on list t − 1, the list in force for those games. Growth applies every month, whether or not the player played, as Glicko's rating deviation does; an inactive player's variance grows until it reaches s_0², the newcomer's.
- **The certainty for a period.** For the games of the period that list t + 1 will rate, played while list t is in force, σ_i² = min(P(t) + c², s_0²). It is known at list t.

## 4 K (R16)

> K_i(n) = clip(q · σ_i² / (κ_tc · (1 + n · q² · σ_i² · v_tc)), K_min, K_max), K_min = 10, K_max = 40,

rounded to one decimal half up and applied to every game of the period, with σ_i from §3, v_tc in the band of the rating on list t and n the period's rated games in tc. The published form of D-0009 (reading 3) is the same number: K_i(n) = clip(C_tc(L)/(N_i + n), K_min, K_max), C_tc(L) = 1/(κ_tc · q · v_tc(L)), N_i = 1/(q² · σ_i² · v_tc(L)). With n = 1 it is R6's K.

## 5 Choosing the growth scale c_K (fixed before any fit)

- Grid, per time control: c_K ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6}.
- Months: carry months 2023-01 to 2024-11 and forecast months 2023-02 to 2024-12, all before E6's first test month (2025-01), as SPEC-L1 §4.6 chose Layer 1's hyperparameters on 2024's last months.
- Criterion: the mean three-outcome log-loss of every forecast game of the time control under the harness of §6, with K_i of §4 for every player of the population.
- The value with the lowest mean log-loss is chosen. A choice at either end of the grid is reported as a flag; the grid is not widened after the fit.

## 6 The test: exactly as E6 tested rung 4

The harness is E6's (`analysis/e6_rungs_extract.py`, rung 4), with its functions imported unchanged, and K_i of §4 in place of R6's:

- **Months.** Carry months 2025-01 to 2026-08; forecast months 2025-02 to 2026-09.
- **Population.** In carry month t, each player of a broadcast game in which both players are rated on the list in force and the player has a published K, and who has at least one Layer 1 game (any time control) in the months from max(2023-01, t − 36) to t − 1: the players for whom E6 had a Layer 1 estimate, since E6's rolling fit indexed exactly those players (`layer1.fit.build`). The same test over every such player without the last condition is reported as a robustness check; the verdict uses the pre-registered population.
- **Carry and forecast.** Each player's published rating is carried through the player's games of month t, once with FIDE's K (the published K of the list in force, without the 700 rule, as E6) and once with K_i(n); month t + 1's games are forecast from the carried ratings with Layer 0's expected score (table 8.1.2 with the 400-point rule and the 2650 exemption, `src/layer0/`), and the three-outcome scores split it with the draw rate of the game's level band in the 12 months before.
- **n.** The larger of the player's games field in tc on list t + 1 (the games the period actually held) and the player's broadcast games of month t in tc.
- **Targeted group.** The forecast games of the players whose |K_i − K| is in the top quarter of the month.
- **Rules (annex T8.1, T8.2).** Targeted: the log-loss of the targeted games better than Layer 0's, with the 95 % interval excluding zero. Do no harm: the upper end of the 95 % interval of the log-loss difference on every forecast game at most 0.002 nats a game, and the game-weighted mean absolute calibration residual over 50-point gap bins at most 0.005 worse. Intervals: paired moving-block bootstrap by month, blocks of 3, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261009 (annex T8.5).
- **Verdict.** Rung 4 passes when both rules hold (D-0009, reading 8).

## 7 What E8 reports

- The chosen c_K per time control, with the log-loss over the grid on the choice months.
- The test: targeted and overall log-loss and Brier differences with intervals, calibration, the verdict; the same for the robustness population.
- The K distribution in the test months, by rating band and by FIDE's K, against R6's K of E6.
- **Established players' K at the October 2026 list**, by K_i(n) for n = 1, 4 and 9 and for the n of their last rated period: established elite players (standard, rated 2600 or more, aged 20 or more, at least 30 rated standard games on the lists since February 2015, at least one in the last 12 lists) and established club adults (standard, rated 1600–1999, aged 25–60, the same activity conditions).
- Stability: the absolute change from one month's broadcast games for adults with FIDE's K of 10 or 20, with FIDE's K and with K_i (E6 §3's measure).

## 8 Acceptance tests (written before the code; synthetic data; run by check (b))

| Test | Criterion |
|---|---|
| KA-1 | K_i(n) lies in [K_min, K_max], is non-increasing in n and non-decreasing in σ; with n = 1 it equals R6's K (`layer1.outputs.k_from_sigma`); before clipping it equals C/(N + n) |
| KA-2 | Idle months add c² to the variance until it reaches s_0²; a period's G games add G · I to the precision |
| KA-3 | With G games every month the recursion converges to the closed-form steady state P⁻ = c² (1 + √(1 + 4/(c² G I)))/2 |
| KA-4 | First appearance: the newcomer's variance uses max(5, G) games from s_0; a player on the first archived list starts from s_list; a re-entry after an absence restarts as a newcomer |
| KA-5 | Ages: the growth follows the profile of SPEC-L1 §3.7 by age band; no year of birth counts as 25–45 |
| KA-6 | The loader refuses a list later than 2026-10 |

## 9 Limits

- The lists count rated games, not opponents: each game is counted at equal strength, which overstates the information of games against much weaker or stronger opponents and so understates σ_i for players who meet them.
- The recursion has no drift: a junior's improvement enters only through the larger growth of the young age bands.
- The games field counts games rated in a period, which can lag the games themselves by a period or two (§7.1.3, §9.1 [V 1]).
- Players rated before February 2015 start from s_list, a stated substitution for a history the archive does not hold.
- σ_i here measures how much FIDE's record says about a player, not how well a model fits the player's results: it is not a Layer 1 posterior, and it carries none of Layer 1's information about whom the player met.
