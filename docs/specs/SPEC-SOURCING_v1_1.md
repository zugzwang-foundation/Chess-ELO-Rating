## 1 What the sample is for

Most rated games are played below 2000: of the player-games of 2025 in the frame, 76 % are those of players rated below 2000 on the October 2025 list, while the broadcast games behind the proposal's fair expectation table sit at a typical 2113 [E2]. The sample turns the pilot's first looks (E21 §5.4) into club-level findings on four questions: the fair table (the favourite's score against table 8.1.2, and against the fitted tables, none fitted on these games), White's edge at equal ratings, adults' results against juniors, and newcomers' first year. Tier 0, on the pages already held, settles what it can of the pilot's list-change puzzle (§8).

## 2 The frame

- **Players:** the pilot's frame: the active players (no inactivity flag) of FIDE's October 2025 standard list in E21's 75 federations (every federation with at least 1,000 rated players on that list, plus England, Scotland and Wales): 168,055 players.
- **Pages:** each 2025 standard rating period in which the list shows games for the player and the player was rated on the list before: 618,801 pages, 2,955,027 player-games by FIDE's lists.
- **Not in the frame:** any period outside 2025; rapid and blitz; a period in which the player was not yet rated on the list before (the games that make a first rating).

## 3 Strata

- **Level:** the rating on the October 2025 list: 1400–1599, 1600–1799, 1800–1999, 2000–2199, and a small stratum of 2200 or more, the anchor to the broadcast evidence.
- **Group:** juniors as E6 defines them, list year minus year of birth at most 19 (born 2006 or later); newcomers, first rated on a list from January 2023 (on none of FIDE's lists from February 2015 to December 2022), who are not juniors; others, adults with an older rating or without a year of birth (annex T2.2: no year of birth counts as an adult). At 2200 or more newcomers are too few to stand apart (6) and are drawn with the others.
- **The draw:** in each stratum, players drawn without replacement, all with the same chance, across every federation of the frame (the exponential race on fixed pseudo-random numbers of SHA-256, seed ELO-D2, as the pilot drew); each player's page a 2025 period drawn at random among the player's own; one page per player. A page already in the cache (the pilot's) is not fetched again.

## 4 Pages, the parser and the opponents

- **The page:** FIDE's per-player calculation page (`/a_indv_calculation.php`, as `tools/data/fetch_fide_calculations.py` requests it, with the same headers), fetched by `tools/data/fetch_sourcing.py`, modes club-robots, club-plan and club-run.
- **The parser** (pilot-informed; `analysis/e21_club_common.py`): E17's, extended to read a tournament whose player FIDE prints with Ro 0 and without K (the pilot's seven pages that E17's parser rejects) and a game row printed without a colour mark, each counted apart. On every pilot page E17's parser reads, the extended parser gives the same tournaments and the same marked games. A row without a colour mark counts as a game and is left out of every measure that uses colour.
- **The opponent** (pilot-informed): identified on FIDE's list in force at the tournament's start, as E21 does, by name and federation, with the rating FIDE prints within 50 points (beyond it on the same side for a row FIDE marks under the 400-point rule), the nearest if several, else on the lists up to three months either side. On the pilot's pages it identifies 11,254 of 11,534 game rows (97.6 %); E21's rule, which asks the exact rating, identified 8,508 of 11,501, because the ratings FIDE prints on its pages today often differ by a few points from the lists as published. The opponent's year of birth is read from FIDE's list.

## 6 Requests and pace

- One process, the pilot's tool and headers, the project's User-Agent, never disguised; at least four seconds after the last request to any host of fide.com, all of fide.com counted as one site, measured from an upper bound of its logged time in this session's log and the pilot's; every page cached once.
- On HTTP 429 or 5xx, or a timeout, the gap doubles; three refusals in a row stop the run, and a page that looks like a block stops it at once; the stop is recorded and nothing restarts on its own.
- Every request logged in `data/fetch_log/ELO-D2.tsv` (UTC time, host, path, status, size, SHA-256); the run stops at 5,500 requests in it. 2025 rating periods only; nothing whose name refers to a 2026 U.S. Championship; no broadcast file. At the pilot's pace, one page every 5.02 s, the run takes about 7.7 hours.

## 7 The analyses, fixed before the main fetch

Each on the sample's pages, each figure with a 95 % interval clustered over pages, weighted to the frame (§7.5) with the unweighted figure beside it; every rating comparison carries the line "Under this rule, with the same results — not anyone's true strength." A cell is shown from 100 games. None is a test of a rung, which stays for FIDE's data.

### 7.1 The fair table at club level

- **Games:** every game row whose opponent's rating FIDE prints uncapped and whose gap is below 400, the two ratings as FIDE prints them (the player's Ro and the opponent's rating); the favourite is the higher-rated player, White at equal ratings (E2's rule); a row is one game from the sampled player's side.
- **Level of the game:** the mean of the two ratings, in E2's 200-point bands: 1400–1599, 1600–1799, 1800–1999, 2000–2199, 2200 or more. **Gap:** 50-point bins from 0–49 to 350–399.
- **E2's measure:** the favourite's score minus table 8.1.2's expectation (FIDE's PD) by gap, every level pooled, and by level band × gap.
- **The fitted tables, none fitted on these games** (E11's and E18's held-out rules): the 2025 self-calibrating table [E18], fitted on broadcast games of March to December 2024; and the v2 table [E11], its rolling fit for the month in which the tournament started, fitted on the 36 months of broadcast games before it (for a tournament that started before January 2025, January 2025's fit). Each with its colour term, at the midpoint of the 100-point band of the game's mean rating, as E11 and E18 apply them; rows without a colour mark left out. The favourite's residual under each by gap, by level band × gap, and pooled by level band.
- **E11's rule (a)**, on the cells of 200-point level band × 50-point gap with at least 1,000 games: a cell is outside when its residual is significantly outside ±0.01 (one-sided test against the nearer bound, standard error clustered over pages, Holm–Bonferroni across the cells of each table); the count of cells outside for each table (E11's 100-point bands would hold too few games here).

### 7.2 White moves first

- **E2's measure:** White's score in games between players at most 25 points apart, by the game's level band; rows without a colour mark left out.
- **The fitted tables' colour term:** at the same games, White's score minus each fitted table's expectation for White (colour included), by level band; and, over every game of §7.1, White's edge left by each table: half the difference of the mean residual as White and as Black. The same edge under table 8.1.2 (FIDE's printed change before K), by level band, as E21 §5.4 measures it.

### 7.3 Juniors

- **E6's measure:** adults' score minus FIDE's expectation against juniors, and against adults at the same gap, by the adult's band below 2000 (1400–1599, 1600–1799, 1800–1999; 2000–2199 and 2200 or more beside them). Juniors and adults as E6 defines them (§3), the opponent's year of birth from FIDE's list once the opponent is identified (§4); a game whose opponent is not identified is left out.
- **The adults' games against juniors**, from the adult's side, read on the adult's page or on the junior's: on the adult's page the residual is FIDE's printed change before K, on the junior's its negative (table 8.1.2's two columns sum to one), and the adult's band is that of the rating FIDE uses for the adult in the game. Rows FIDE marks under the 400-point rule, and tournaments printed without K, are left out.
- **The matched control** (E6): every game of an adult against an adult read on an adult's page, from the sampled adult's side, binned by colour and 50-point gap (own rating minus the opponent's); each game of an adult against a junior is compared with the control's mean residual in its own bin, and the junior-specific residual is the difference. Rows without a colour mark stay out of the control and of the difference.

### 7.4 Newcomers

- **Players first rated on a list from April 2024** (E21 §3.3), on the sample's pages of periods within 12 months after the first rating: score minus FIDE's expectation (the printed change before K), by age when first rated (under 12, 12–15, 16–19, 20–24, 25–45, 46–60, over 60) and over every age. Positive means the newcomers score more than FIDE's expectation from their ratings: rated behind their results in that year.

### 7.5 Weights and intervals

- **Weights:** a page stands for the frame players of its stratum over the sampled players of the stratum, times the player's 2025 frame pages (its period was drawn at random among them), as in the pilot; a game row carries its page's weight. The weighted figure is the frame's; the unweighted, beside it, is the sample's, in which juniors and newcomers are drawn three times as often.
- **Intervals:** 95 %, the ratio estimator's linearised variance clustered over pages (one page per player); games on two sampled pages are few and each counts on its own page.

## 8 Tier 0: the pages already held

- **The parser fix** (§4), on the pilot's 2,000 pages: the rows printed without K and the row without a colour mark, counted.
- **The list-change puzzle:** on the pilot's pages whose published list change differs from the page's rounded total, with the player's Ro equal to the list before and the page's games equal to the list's (E21 §5.2), the known rules are tested first against the pages and the lists: the cap on K times the games of a period (R-23), the rating floor (R-32), games rated in a later period (R-09, R-10), games against players unrated when the event began (R-12), and rounding per tournament or once per period (R-25), each against SPEC-L0 v1.5's citation of the regulations; then, decided after seeing the pilot, the ratings as published instead of as FIDE's pages print them today. How many each explains, and what remains; nothing is claimed about FIDE beyond what the pages and lists show, and what stays unexplained is NOT VERIFIED.

## 9 Terms, privacy, deletion and the lock

- FIDE's footer reserves storing its pages to its written permission (`docs/research/SOURCES_v1_0.md`); no request is made to FIDE or to anyone in this session (item 34). Pages stay in `data/` (gitignored); only totals by level, age group and colour, with their intervals, are committed: no sampled player's name, FIDE ID or row. Many sampled players are minors.
- **Deletion** (item 35): once the session's analysis is verified, the raw pages of the pilot and of this run, and every per-game file derived from them, are deleted; a local manifest, `data/raw/sourcing/MANIFEST`, keeps each page's link, period and SHA-256, so that anyone can fetch it again. E17's and E20's caches are not touched.
- **The lock:** 2025 rating periods only; nothing whose name refers to a 2026 U.S. Championship; no broadcast-archive file; no result of either 2026 U.S. Championship read; the freeze-3d files unchanged.
