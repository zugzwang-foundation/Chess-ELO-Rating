# SPEC-SOURCING — a larger fetch of FIDE's per-player game pages: the draft plan

Status: DRAFT — pending the founder's decision (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 29); nothing here is run until the founder decides. Written in session ELO-D1 (14 October 2026) from the pilot's results; generated by `analysis/e21_sourcing_report.py --spec` from the aggregates E21 uses, so that every number is the pilot's or FIDE's lists'; do not edit by hand. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (`docs/LICENSE-docs.md`). The brief that this plan serves, and the recommendation, are E21 §7 [E21]. Choices marked **pilot-informed** were set after the pilot's pages had been seen.

## 1 What the fetch is for

Rated games of the whole pool, with colours, the opponent's rating and federation, and FIDE's own change, at the levels the broadcast archive misses: for E7's federation offsets, E2's and E11's calibration by gap, level and colour, and E6's measure of adults' results against juniors (§8).

## 2 The sampling frame

- **Players:** the active players (no inactivity flag) of FIDE's October 2025 standard list, of every federation with at least 1,000 rated players on that list, plus England, Scotland and Wales (75 federations): 168,055 players.
- **Pages:** each 2025 standard rating period in which the player played a rated game and was rated on the list before: 618,801 pages, 2,955,027 player-games by FIDE's lists.
- **Not in the frame:** periods after the October 2026 list, events that ended on or after 1 October 2026 or that name a 2026 U.S. Championship (the championship lock); rapid and blitz; a period in which the player was not yet rated on the list before (the games that make a first rating).

## 3 Strata and sizes

- **Strata:** federation × level band (below 1400, 1400–1599, …, 2200–2399, 2400 or more, on the October 2025 list) × group (juniors born 2007 or later, or newcomers first rated 2023 or later; others).
- **Sizes, the smaller run (pilot-informed):** per federation, the pages that bring 300 cross-border player-games on its own players' pages at the pilot's rate for that federation (E7's threshold), and at least 400, the smallest floor in steps of 100 at which the projected games of adults against juniors reach 1,000 in each level band below 2000; at most 2,000, never more than its 2025 pages: about 33,037 pages; 75 of 75 federations reach 300 on their own pages and 12 reach 1,000 (E21 §7 gives each). Within a federation, as the pilot: every level band from the floor up, in proportion to the square root of the band's players, at least one page each, juniors and newcomers drawn three times as often as others; one page per player before any player gets a second.
- **What the smaller run would bring** (pilot-informed, the pilot's rates scaled federation by federation): 185,740 player-games, 51,180 of them cross-border; 39 of the 44 cells of 100-point level band × 50-point gap below 2000 that the rating floor allows with 1,000 games or more (the smallest 30); games of adults against juniors by the adult's level band: 1400–1599 1,165; 1600–1799 2,436; 1800–1999 4,063; 2000–2199 2,661; 2200–2399 2,572; 2400 or more 2,300; games between players at most 50 points apart, by level: 1400–1599 5,951; 1600–1799 7,248; 1800–1999 6,907; 2000–2199 3,187; 2200–2399 2,657; 2400 or more 1,948.
- **Sizes, the full fetch:** every frame page, 618,801.
- **Weights:** a page stands for the frame players of its stratum over the sampled ones, times the player's 2025 pages, as in the pilot.

## 4 The route

- **Per-player calculation pages** (`/a_indv_calculation.php`, as `tools/data/fetch_fide_calculations.py` requests them, read by E17's parser): the only FIDE page that gives individual games with colours (E21 §5).
- **Tournament reports** as an index only (`/report.phtml`; pilot-informed, E21 §6): they give no game, but every player's FIDE ID per event, so that opponents can be identified exactly and whole events taken where a measure needs them. One request per event.
- **The parser** (pilot-informed): E17's, extended to accept the game rows FIDE prints without K (7 pages of the pilot's 2,000) and without a colour mark (1 of the 11,502 games on the pages read), each counted apart.
- **FIDE's tournament-report archive**, requested of FIDE by the proposal, replaces both if FIDE shares it.

## 5 Requests and pace

- One request per page; one process; at least four seconds after the last request to any host of fide.com, from an upper bound of its logged time; the pilot ran at one page every 5.02 s (the pilot's pace; pilot-informed).
- The smaller run: about 33,037 requests, 1.9 days without a pause at that pace. The full fetch: 618,801 requests, 36 days.
- Run in stretches the founder sets (for example a few hours a day), each logged, resumable from its queue; on HTTP 429 or 5xx the gap doubles, three refusals in a row stop the run, a page that looks like a block stops it at once, and the founder is told before anything restarts. The client is never disguised.

## 6 Deduplication

- A game between two players whose pages are both fetched appears twice. In the pilot 71 % of game rows had an opponent who is a frame player with a page for the same period (pilot-informed); in a sample of pages the overlap is small, while in the full fetch each of those games appears twice.
- Key: the event, the two players' FIDE IDs (the opponent's from the event's report, or by name, federation and rating on the list in force), the colours (White's page marks White) and the score; a pair that meets twice in one event is kept twice only if both pages show it twice.
- Each measure counts a game once: E7 and E2/E11 per distinct game; E6 from the adult's side.

## 7 What counts as enough

- **A federation's offset (E7):** at least 300 cross-border games for an estimate (E7's threshold), at least 1,000 for a headline (Ghita's floor, p. 65); reported as a direction with its interval.
- **Calibration (E2, E11):** cells of level band × 50-point gap with at least 1,000 games, E11's rule (a); White's edge by 200-point level band from at least 1,000 games between players at most 50 points apart (E2 uses 25).
- **The junior measure (E6):** at least 1,000 games of adults against juniors in each level band below 2000.

## 8 The analyses a run would do

- **E7's offsets** on the run's cross-border games, weighted and recursive as E7 (and Ghita) compute them, by federation, with intervals; compared, by direction only, with E7's broadcast offsets.
- **E2's and E11's calibration** of table 8.1.2 and of the fitted table by gap, level and colour, with their rules, on the pool's games of 2025.
- **E6's junior measure:** adults' score minus expectation against juniors, against a matched control of adult games at the same gaps, by level band.
- Each is labelled as decided after seeing the pilot's data; none is a test of a rung, which stays for FIDE's data (the proposal's tests on FIDE's data).

## 9 Terms, privacy and the lock

- FIDE's footer reserves storing its pages to its written permission; the request goes to FIDE before or with any larger run (E21 §7). Pages stay in `data/` (gitignored); only totals by federation, level, age group and colour are committed; no player's name, FIDE ID or row (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 31). Many of the players are minors.
- The championship lock holds: no period after the October 2026 list, no event ended on or after 1 October 2026, nothing naming a 2026 U.S. Championship, no new broadcast file.
