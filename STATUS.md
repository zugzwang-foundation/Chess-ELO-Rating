# Status

Status: DRAFT — where the project stands, in plain words; updated at every session close-out. Nothing here is final or published. Last updated 11 October 2026.

## Done

- **An exact, open copy of FIDE's rating rules** (Layer 0, `src/layer0/`). Its specification is ratified. It reproduces FIDE's own published calculations: every game and player of the 2025 U.S. Championship, and 56 of 58 multi-event rating periods (two are one point off, unexplained and disclosed). Earlier versions of the rules are coded beside it: 2020–2026 and 2008–2019.
- **Evidence reports E0 to E20** (`docs/evidence/`), each generated by a committed script. They cover FIDE's monthly lists since 2015 and over-the-board broadcast games since 2020. Topics include deflation, junior lag, federation residuals and the expected-score table. They also cover a simulator, today's top 100 replayed since 2020, and the leading players since 1971 on FIDE's own data.
- **The proposal, its technical annex and a short brief**, version 1.0 (`docs/proposal/`). Seven changes ("rungs") are offered, each adoptable alone, with a verdict for each from the evidence.
- **A comparison on the 2026 U.S. Championships, fixed before any result was read.** It was frozen in stages, each recorded in a decision record and a git tag, and the automated check fails if a frozen file changes. It includes a forecast test whose reading was fixed in advance.
- **Records**: 17 decision records, with the architect's rulings R1–R79, and a red-team review of every working session (`docs/decisions/`, `docs/review/`).
- **Checks**: 672 automated tests. Every number in the documents is regenerated and compared on every pull request.

## Next

- **After FIDE's 1 November 2026 list:** check Layer 0 against FIDE's published calculations for both events. This check decides the first rung's verdict. Two small items wait for it: the reading of one K rule in the specification, and a docstring in the engine.
- **Open technical questions from the latest review:**
  - how FIDE's lists rounded half points between 2008 and 2018;
  - how the backward-applied table's headline is presented;
  - ties in the selection of the leading players;
  - the terms attached to some historical data;
  - from which year a new rule for choosing the table's form applies;
  - one list on which two FIDE sources disagree.
- **Later:** each year's table is refitted only on earlier games, under the rules already written (`docs/specs/SPEC-TABLE-FIT_v1_2.md`, `docs/specs/SPEC-SELFCAL_v1_2.md`). The rungs marked "test on FIDE data" need FIDE's tournament-report archive, which the proposal requests.
