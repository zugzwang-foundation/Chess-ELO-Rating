# Status

Status: DRAFT — where the project stands, in plain words; updated at every session close-out. Nothing here is final or published. Last updated 12 October 2026.

## Done

- **An exact, open copy of FIDE's rating rules** (Layer 0, `src/layer0/`). Its specification is ratified. It reproduces FIDE's own published calculations: every game and player of the 2025 U.S. Championship, and 56 of 58 multi-event rating periods (two are one point off, unexplained and disclosed). Earlier versions of the rules are coded beside it: 2020–2026 and 2008–2019.
- **Evidence reports E0 to E20** (`docs/evidence/`), each generated by a committed script. They cover FIDE's monthly lists since 2015 and over-the-board broadcast games since 2020. Topics include deflation, junior lag, federation residuals and the expected-score table. They also cover a simulator, today's top 100 replayed since 2020, and the leading players since 1971 on FIDE's own data.
- **The proposal, its technical annex and a short brief**, version 1.0 (`docs/proposal/`). Seven changes ("rungs") are offered, each adoptable alone, with a verdict for each from the evidence.
- **A comparison on the 2026 U.S. Championships, fixed before any result was read.** It was frozen in stages, each recorded in a decision record and a git tag, and the automated check fails if a frozen file changes. It includes a forecast test whose reading was fixed in advance.
- **Records**: 18 decision records, with the architect's rulings R1–R89, kept as history, and a red-team review of every working session to 11 October (`docs/decisions/`, `docs/review/`). From 12 October decisions go one dated line each in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **Data terms**: the figures derived from OlimpBase's transcription of FIDE's lists carry its non-commercial terms, and README's licence section names every file that holds them. FIDE's own material is analysed locally; what is committed of it is aggregates and differences, test fixtures quoting FIDE's published calculations, three event files, short quotations of FIDE's texts and single published ratings where a table needs them (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, item 6).
- **Checks**: 686 automated tests. Every number in the documents is regenerated and compared on every pull request.
- **Light mode** (12 October): fewer process gates, the same checks. Decisions are one dated line each in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, mistakes are fixed in place with a dated line, and the proposal and brief get one review before submission (`CLAUDE.md`, "How we work").
- **Corrections** (12 October): E20 reads its check of the two tables on the greats' games by when the players were chosen, and says nowhere that FIDE's table fits those games better; Appendix F marks which rounding text was in force in 2018 as NOT VERIFIED; five statements of the decision record D-0018 are corrected in place; a test fixture's identifiers that the test does not use are placeholders.
- **The quality pass** (12 October): the proposal's summary opens with what FIDE gets at once, and rung 2's benefit is one figure from the simulator; ruling numbers and freeze vocabulary are gone from the body; Sonas's and Regan's prior work is credited, with seven sources checked; Appendix C keeps only the sources the documents rely on, without duplicates.
